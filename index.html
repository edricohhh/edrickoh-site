Hello, I’m Edric.

I’ve been capturing moments for over five years, and photography has become both my passion and purpose. Whether it’s a corporate event, a store opening, or a birthday celebration, I strive to preserve each moment with authenticity and artistry. It’s always a pleasure to work with you and turn your memories into timeless visuals.

I shoot mostly natural light, I direct gently when you need it, and I stay out of the way when you don’t. If you’re nervous in front of a camera — most people are — that’s genuinely the part of the job I enjoy most.
